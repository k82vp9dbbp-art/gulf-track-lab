Data and calculation notes

Storm winds are storm-wide maximum sustained winds, not wind speed or gusts at the house. Wind footprints use each member’s quadrant radii and indicate modeled threshold coverage; member fractions are not calibrated real-world probabilities.

Between the CSV’s six-hour samples, positions, intensity, pressure, and radii are linearly interpolated. Motion is calculated from the bracketing track segment. The median combines separate medians of the available members at each time; it is a statistical summary, not a physically coherent forecast member. Median footprints combine median radii and position. Local coverage counts always use individual members.

Landfall is the first ocean-to-land crossing of the published Natural Earth 1:10m land geometry, intersected directly with linearly interpolated track segments. This coastline is more detailed than the original sketch but may omit small barrier islands. No crossing means none found within the available forecast and mapped region. Missing fields remain unavailable; ended tracks are never extrapolated.
